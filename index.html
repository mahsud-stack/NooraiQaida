Noorani Qaida

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Noorani Qaida is a Qur'an-reading course app. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information — which, short version, is nothing: it doesn't collect it in the first place.

What this app does

Noorani Qaida teaches the traditional Noorani Qaida — the letters, vowel signs, and reading rules that come before the Qur'an itself — entirely on your device. There's no server behind it. Every lesson, every sound file, and every font it uses is bundled with the app, so it works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. Noorani Qaida has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We don't know who you are, and we have no way to find out.

There is no backend server for the app to send anything to, even if it wanted to.

What's stored on your device

Your lesson progress and preferences — which letters you've practised, your chosen theme, font, reciter, and language — are saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device app storage. This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings, erases it completely.

Microphone access

Some lessons offer an optional "record yourself" button, so you can compare your own pronunciation against the reference audio. When you use it:

  • The recording is held only in your device's memory for that practice session.
  • It is never uploaded, transmitted, or saved to persistent storage anywhere — including by us.
  • It's discarded automatically the moment you re-record, move to another item, or close the app.

If you don't use this feature, the microphone is never accessed.

Audio playback

Recitation audio is either a recording bundled inside the app itself, or, where a recording isn't yet available, your device's own built-in text-to-speech voice. Neither route sends anything to an external server that we control.

App permissions

Third-party services

None. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash reporting, no third party of any kind has access to anything you do in this app.

Children's privacy

Noorani Qaida is often used by children learning to read the Qur'an, typically alongside a parent or teacher. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone — adult or child — there is nothing to protect beyond what's already described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that works differently — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
